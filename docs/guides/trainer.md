# Trainer walkthrough

You coach the Fellows. The pictures of children are the pupils those Fellows will teach. You see only the cohorts an admin put you on; another trainer's cohort is not in your list. An admin adds cohorts, steps and courses. You edit the modules, lesson files and quizzes of the courses on your cohorts.

Primary trainers cover playgroup through Standard 7. Secondary trainers cover Form I.

The words: a **cohort** is a group of Fellows. It has **steps**, a step holds **courses**, a course has **modules**, and a module has **lesson files**. A **Fellow** is a learner in the academy.

## A. Sign in

Open the Trainer tab and sign in. One browser holds one sign-in at a time: signing in as someone else replaces the earlier session. The first visit starts a short tour. **Tour** in the header plays it again.

## B. Your desk

You land on **Desk**. The numbers across the top are yours: cohorts, Fellows, progress, quizzes, assessments, attendance, and upcoming sessions. Each opens the tab it counts. The side bar has Desk, Cohorts, Assessments & quizzes, Progress, Attendance, Messages, and Schedule. **Approvals** is a link in the header.

## C. Open a cohort

Choose **Cohorts** and pick the cohort. The name is the class those Fellows are learning to teach, for example **Playgroup — Montessori** or **Secondary — Form I**. The page opens on Progress for that cohort. The courses placed on the cohort are linked under its name, and the other cohorts you run are one click away along the row below it.

The tabs on a cohort:

| Tab | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Progress | See the cohort overview, and the course percent each Fellow sees. **Add Fellows** by ID, name or email. Write a feedback comment, or take a Fellow out of the cohort with the remove button. An admin's step save adds Fellows and never removes one, so a Fellow you add here stays in the cohort until someone removes them. |
| Assessments & quizzes | Upload an assessment, write an exam, or upload a paper exam. Assign a module quiz from a lesson file. Tick Fellows to give it only to them. Leave the boxes empty to give it to the whole cohort. An assigned quiz or exam allows three attempts. Reset a Fellow's attempts when they have used all three. |
| Attendance | Mark who came. |
| Schedule | Put a session or a deadline on the calendar. |

**Messages** is a tab of its own: a short 1:1 note to a Fellow, and everything that reaches you.

Certificates are not a tab. An admin issues them (section F).

**Open cohort room** is the class office: roster import, written work, marking, the calendar, and a note to the whole cohort.

## D. Build the course

An admin adds a course and places it on a step of your cohort. You do not add or delete courses.

Open a course from the link under the cohort name. That page lists its modules. You can add a module, add or replace its lesson files, and edit the quiz for each lesson file.

A course is one stage. A module inside it is one focus, such as Practical life or Kiswahili. A lesson file sits inside the module. The course page reminds you to plan each module as Introduction, Competence development, Design, then Realisation. Playgroup uses the five Montessori areas instead of that subject list.

Draft courses stay hidden from Fellows. Published courses appear on their desk once an admin has placed them on their cohort.

**Quizzes.** An AI draft is saved as unapproved. A Fellow sees a quiz only after a person approves it. You approve the quizzes of the courses on your own cohorts, from the course page. A lesson file with no approved quiz does not block a Fellow: it counts as done once they mark it read. A new lesson file therefore has no quiz until someone approves one. The pass mark for a module quiz is 80%.

**Order.** The admin's step order sets the course order. A course opens for a Fellow when the course before it in step order is complete.

## E. Let someone in

**Approvals** accepts a new Fellow. You cannot create an admin, clear the academy, or open another trainer's cohort. The trainer desk has no password reset; ask an admin. An admin puts you on a cohort.

## F. Certificates

You do not issue certificates. An admin does, by hand, and only after the Fellow has completed every module of the course. Automatic certificates are not shipped.

## G. A normal week

1. Confirm the Fellows are in your cohort.
2. Confirm the course for each step is placed.
3. Add or check the lesson file on the current module, and approve its quiz.
4. Assign a quiz or an exam, or set a written task in the cohort room.
5. Put the live session on the calendar.
6. After they work, read progress, mark written work, and mark attendance.
7. Write a feedback comment when one Fellow needs a note that is not a score.
8. Send a cohort note when the whole class needs the same message.
