# Fellow guide

You are a Fellow: a learner in the academy, training to teach. Sign in on the Fellow tab with your username, Fellow ID, or email. One browser holds one sign-in at a time: signing in as someone else replaces the earlier session. **Help** in the header replays the short tour.

You see only your own cohorts, your own courses, and your own marks.

## How the academy is laid out

You are in a **cohort**. A cohort has **steps**, and a step holds **courses**. A course has **modules**, and a module has **lesson files**. Your admin sets the order of the steps, and that order is the order of your courses: a course opens when the course before it is complete.

## The desk

The header holds the logo, "Silverleaf Academy" and a **Fellow** pill. On the right are **Help**, **Settings**, the bell (a blue number means unread notes), the light and dark switch, your name and Fellow ID, and **Log out**. The side menu runs down the left and has eight names. On a narrower screen it moves above the page. On a phone it is a bottom bar with Home, Learning, Work and Messages, and **More** opens Quizzes, Progress, Reflect and Certificates; the header shows your initials, and tapping them opens Help, the switch and Log out. There are no pictures on any page.

## What you do in a module

A module opens when you have finished the module before it.

1. Read the objective. If the module has lesson files, open each one and read it.
2. If a lesson file has an approved quiz, take it. You pass at 80%. Once you pass, the lesson file is done. After each try you see which of your answers were right; the correct answers appear only once you pass.
3. If a lesson file has no approved quiz yet, mark it read. It is done once you do.
4. A module with no lesson file counts as done without opening it, so it never holds you up. A course with no lesson files at all cannot be marked complete yet; wait for your trainer to add them.
5. When every lesson file in the module is done, the next module opens.

Lesson quizzes can be retaken. An assigned quiz or exam from your trainer allows three attempts. If you miss it twice, a notice asks you to open the module again before the last attempt. Your trainer can reset your attempts.

**A finished exam.** An exam is finished when you have passed it or used all your tries. Opening it from Quizzes then shows your tries and your best score, with **Review answers** as the button, instead of a new try.

## Finishing a course

A course is complete when every module in it is complete. When it is, **Mark complete and open the next** appears on the Learning page. It stays hidden until every module is done, and the server refuses it before then. Marking it opens the next course in your cohort's step order.

## Certificates

An admin issues certificates. Nothing is issued automatically (not shipped). A course certificate needs every module of that course complete. A Fellowship or Advanced Level certificate needs you to have finished the cohorts the academy path asks for. Open **Certificates** in the side menu to see the ones issued to you. **Progress** and Certificates both show how many cohorts you have finished on the academy path.

## Module discussion

On a lesson page you can post in **Module discussion**. The other Fellows in your cohort can read it. Your trainer does not see this thread, so use desk mail or written work when you need an answer. A trainer view of the discussion is not shipped.
