# How trainers and teachers talk

They share one batch. They do not share one chat room. Each kind of message has a job.

## They reply to each other

| Point | Who starts | Where | What comes back |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desk mail | Either person | Trainer: under the teacher list. Teacher: top of the desk, with their trainer. | A short 1:1 thread. The other person also gets a bell notice, “New desk message”. |
| Written work | Trainer | Cohort room. The teacher sees it in **My work**. | The teacher submits a written answer. The trainer scores it from 0 to 100 and writes a comment. The teacher sees the mark on **My work** and a bell notice, “Written work marked”. |
| Feedback comment | Trainer | Message icon on the teacher’s card. Categories: progress, improvement, general. | The teacher reads it under **Trainer feedback**. There is no reply box. |
| Batch note | Trainer | Cohort room, **Announce**. | Every teacher in that batch gets it under **From the academy** and in the bell. They do not answer inside the note. |

Use desk mail when one teacher needs a private reply. Use a batch note when the whole class needs the same sentence. Use written work when you need a piece of work and a mark. Use a feedback comment when you are judging progress and do not need an answer.

## They are connected, but it is not a conversation

| Point | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Joining a batch | The teacher gets a notice: “You’re in”. |
| Calendar | The trainer adds a session or a deadline. The teacher sees it on **My calendar** and gets a bell notice. |
| Quiz result | The app tells the teacher the next lesson is open, the next module is open, or the quiz needs another try. The trainer sees the same pass or fail on **Progress**. |
| Progress and attendance | The trainer marks attendance and reads the course percent. The teacher sees the same percent and their own attendance. Nobody types a message. |
| Certificates | A finished course can produce a certificate. Both sides can open it. |
| Reflection | The teacher writes it on their desk. Admin analytics can read it. It does not appear on the trainer’s batch page. |
| Course rating | The teacher scores the course. That score goes to analytics, not into a thread with the trainer. |
| Module discussion | The teacher can post on the lesson page. The trainer’s screens do not open that thread, so a post there does not reach the trainer’s desk. |

## When they need to talk

Talk when the lesson file is missing and the teacher is blocked, when written work is unclear, when a quiz has been missed twice, when attendance does not match the session, and when one teacher needs a different explanation from the rest of the batch.

Do not use talk for a pass mark, a locked module, or a certificate. Those move by themselves once the work is done.
