# Trainer walkthrough

You coach the Fellows. You see only the cohorts an admin put you on; another trainer's cohort is not in your list. An admin adds cohorts, steps and courses. You edit the modules, lesson files and quizzes of the courses on your cohorts.

Primary trainers cover playgroup through Standard 7. Secondary trainers cover Form I.

The words: a **cohort** is a group of Fellows. It has **steps**, a step holds **courses**, a course has **modules**, and a module has **lesson files**. A **Fellow** is a learner in the academy. A **session** is only a meeting on the calendar. Attendance is marked per day, not per session.

## A. Sign in and the frame

Open the Trainer tab and sign in. One browser holds one sign-in at a time: signing in as someone else replaces the earlier session. The first visit starts a short tour. **Help** in the header plays it again.

The header holds the logo, "Silverleaf Academy" and a **Trainer** pill. On the right are **Help**, the bell (a blue number means unread notes), the light and dark switch, your name, and **Log out**. The side menu runs down the left: Home, Cohorts, Fellows, Courses, Assessments, Progress, Attendance, Messages, Schedule, and Calendar. On a narrower screen it moves above the page. On a phone it is a bottom bar with Home, Cohorts, Assessments and Messages, and **More** opens Fellows, Courses, Progress, Attendance, Schedule and Calendar; the header shows your initials, and tapping them opens Help, the switch and Log out. There are no pictures on any page.

## B. Home

You land on **Home**, with **Add a session** at the top. Top to bottom:

- **Needs you today.** One row for each thing waiting, with one button on the row: Fellows waiting for approval (**Review approvals**), a module with no lesson file (**Add a lesson file**), and a Fellow who has not started (**Send a note**). A line under the rows counts quiz answers to mark, sessions today, and approvals. **This term** below counts written answers to mark.
- **Quizzes waiting for your approval.** The newest drafts; **See all** opens Assessments.
- **Your cohorts.** The first four, with their Fellows, steps and status. **See all cohorts** opens Cohorts.
- **This term.** Fellows, quizzes open, assessments, and attendance.

**Approvals** has no name in the side menu. You reach it from Home.

## C. Cohorts

Choose **Cohorts**, find your cohort in the list (there is a **Find a cohort** box), and click it. The name is the group those Fellows belong to, for example **Playgroup — Montessori** or **Secondary — Form I**. The page shows:

- the cohort's name, whether it is **Open** or **Closed**, and whether its quizzes are on or stopped. An admin changes those;
- **Open cohort room** (below);
- **Steps**, in order, and the courses an admin placed on the cohort. Each course is a link to its page (section D);
- **Fellows**, with **Add a Fellow** (by Fellow ID, name or email). Each Fellow has **Message** (desk mail), **Feedback** (a comment they can read but not reply to) and **More actions**: **View details**, **Give a star**, **Disqualify…**, and **Remove from cohort…**.

A Fellow you add stays in the cohort until someone removes them. An admin taking a Fellow off a step does not remove them from the cohort.

Each of the other names in the side menu works across your cohorts:

| Name | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Assessments | Approve the quizzes waiting for you. Under **Exams**, **Upload an assessment**, **Upload a paper exam** or **Write an exam**, and give it to the whole cohort or only to the Fellows you tick. Under **Lesson quizzes on your cohorts**, review them, and assign a **Checkpoint quiz** or a **File quiz**. An assigned quiz or exam allows three tries and passes at 80% unless the exam sets its own mark. **Reset Attempts** lets a Fellow who has used all three try again; open a quiz's Fellow attempts to do it. **Quiz answers to mark** appears when a Fellow wrote an open answer in a quiz; mark it **Pass** or **Fail**. Written assessments are marked from **Mark submissions** instead, and Home counts them as **Written answers to mark**. |
| Progress | Choose a cohort. A table shows each Fellow's status in each course placed on that cohort, and their medals. Click a Fellow's name for details. **Download CSV** saves the gradebook. |
| Attendance | **Mark attendance for today** (or another date): choose the cohort and the date, then mark each Fellow present, late, absent or excused, or use **Set everyone**. **Save attendance** saves the marks for that day. **Recent marks** and **Attendance in figures** follow. |
| Messages | Desk mail with a Fellow, **Send a cohort note** to the whole cohort, **Feedback comments**, and your inbox. |
| Schedule | This week, day by day, with sessions and deadlines. To change a session, use **Calendar → Timetable**: **Edit** or **Remove** on the session, or **Schedule a session**. **Add a session** opens a form: cohort, title, type (Session, Assessment, Deadline, Holiday or Other), start, optional end, and a description. **Add to the schedule** saves it. The **×** removes an entry. |

Certificates are not on your menu. An admin issues them (section F).

**Open cohort room** is the cohort's office. Its tabs are People (the roster, with **Add a Fellow** and **Import a roster (CSV)**), Quizzes, Gradebook, Graduates, Announce (a note to the whole cohort), Calendar, Assessments (set written work, then **Mark submissions**), and Import.

## D. Build the course

An admin adds a course and places it on a step of your cohort, or you make one: **Courses** → **Make a course**, then **Open and add content**, and **Add to a cohort** to put it on one of your cohorts (Fellows see it only once it is on a cohort). You can edit a course you made or one on your cohorts. Only an admin deletes a course.

Uploading: slides, documents, images, audio and video up to 500 MB go straight to storage, so a recorded session can be a lesson file. A video plays in the page.

Open a course from the link on the cohort page. That page lists its modules. You can add a module, add or replace its lesson files, and edit the quiz for each lesson file.

A course is one stage. A module inside it is one focus, such as Practical life or Kiswahili. A lesson file sits inside the module. The course page reminds you to plan each module as Introduction, Competence development, Design, then Realisation. Playgroup uses the five Montessori areas instead of that subject list.

Draft courses stay hidden from Fellows. A course an admin adds is published at once, and appears on a Fellow's desk once an admin has placed it on their cohort.

**Quizzes.** An AI draft is saved as unapproved. A Fellow sees a quiz only after a person approves it. You approve the quizzes of the courses on your own cohorts, from the course page, or from **Quizzes waiting for your approval** on Home and Assessments. A lesson file with no approved quiz does not block a Fellow: it counts as done once they mark it read. A new lesson file therefore has no quiz until someone approves one. The pass mark for a module quiz is 80%.

**Order.** The admin's step order sets the course order. A course opens for a Fellow when the course before it in step order is complete.

## E. Let someone in

**Approvals** accepts a new Fellow (open it from Home). You cannot create an admin, clear the academy, or open another trainer's cohort. The trainer desk has no password reset; ask an admin. An admin puts you on a cohort.

## F. Certificates

You do not issue certificates. An admin does, by hand, and only after the Fellow has completed every module of the course. Automatic certificates are not shipped. You can see the ones issued in a cohort under **Graduates** in the cohort room.

## G. A normal week

1. Open Home and work down **Needs you today**.
2. Confirm the Fellows are in your cohort.
3. Confirm the course for each step is placed.
4. Add or check the lesson file on the current module, and approve its quiz.
5. Assign a quiz or an exam, or set a written task in the cohort room.
6. Put the live session on the calendar (Schedule).
7. After they work, read Progress, mark written work, and mark attendance for the day.
8. Write a feedback comment when one Fellow needs a note that is not a score.
9. Send a cohort note when the whole cohort needs the same message.
