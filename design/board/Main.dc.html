Across every desk

  • Four shells: cream with tabs (admin), navy with no menu (cohort room), navy-to-teal gradient with coloured pills (trainer), blue header with tabs plus a bottom bar (Fellow).
  • Colour carries no meaning: every tile and pill has its own hue, so status has nowhere to go.
  • White text sits on busy photos on the trainer and Fellow desks.
  • Figures show 0 while loading, or when a request is cancelled, instead of a loading state.
  • Icon-only buttons (help, theme, logout) have no accessible names.

How to read this board

  • One board per role: Sign-in, Admin, Trainer and Fellow. Each capture has its findings below it.
  • Orange dots are design findings, from the Design Feedback doc and from this capture pass.
  • Frames titled "Today" are the screens as they are; frames titled "Proposed" are the redesigns. The shared shell sits to the right of this overview. Each row below starts with a Today board, followed by every proposed page for that desk, in menu order: sign-in first, then Admin, Trainer and Fellow.
