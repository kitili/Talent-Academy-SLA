# Fellow guide

You are a Fellow: a teacher in training. The classrooms in the pictures are the pupils you are preparing to teach. Sign in on the Fellow tab with your username, Fellow ID, or email. One browser holds one sign-in at a time: signing in as someone else replaces the earlier session. **Tour** replays the short tour.

You see only your own cohorts, your own courses, and your own marks.

## How the academy is laid out

You are in a **cohort**. A cohort has **steps**, and a step holds **courses**. A course has **modules**, and a module has **lesson files**. Your admin sets the order of the steps, and that order is the order of your courses: a course opens when the course before it is complete.

## The desk

The desk has seven tabs. **Certificates** and **Tour** sit in the header.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Home | **To do** (your next actions), **Continue learning** (opens the next unlocked module), and your goals. |
| Learning | **Cohorts to finish**, **Medals**, and **My learning**: each course with a percent and a status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Passed, Failed, or Locked), and the modules inside it. |
| Work | **My calendar** (sessions and deadlines for your cohorts) and **My work**: written work and assessments your trainer set. You write the answer. The trainer's mark and comment appear on the same card. |
| Quiz | **Your exams**: quizzes and exams your trainer assigned to you. A written exam opens as a document. An uploaded paper opens on the left, and you write your answers in the document on the right. |
| Progress | Your level, quizzes, average score, modules and reflections, and your progress toward graduation. |
| Reflect | Goals, **Course reflection** (your note after a module, with an optional rating), **Rate your courses**, and **Trainer feedback**: comments your trainer wrote about you. You can read them. You do not reply on that card. |
| Messages | Desk mail with your trainer, and **From the academy**: notices from trainers and admin. |

## What you do in a module

A module opens when you have finished the module before it.

1. Read the objective. If the module has lesson files, open each one and read it.
2. If a lesson file has an approved quiz, take it. You pass at 80%. Once you pass, the lesson file is done. You see which answers were right only after you pass.
3. If a lesson file has no approved quiz yet, mark it read. It is done once you do.
4. A module with no lesson file counts as done without opening it.
5. When every lesson file in the module is done, the next module opens.

Lesson quizzes can be retaken. An assigned quiz or exam from your trainer allows three attempts. If you miss it twice, a notice asks you to open the module again before the last attempt. Your trainer can reset your attempts.

## Finishing a course

A course is complete when every module in it is complete. When it is, **Mark complete and open the next** appears on the Learning tab. It stays hidden until every module is done, and the server refuses it before then. Marking it opens the next course in your cohort's step order.

## Certificates

An admin issues certificates. Nothing is issued automatically (not shipped). A course certificate needs every module of that course complete. A Fellowship or Advanced Level certificate needs you to have finished the cohorts the academy path asks for. Open **Certificates** in the header to see the ones issued to you. **Cohorts to finish** on the Learning tab shows how many cohorts you have completed.

## Module discussion

On a lesson page you can post in **Module discussion**. The other Fellows in your cohort can read it. Your trainer does not see this thread, so use desk mail or written work when you need an answer. A trainer view of the discussion is not shipped.
